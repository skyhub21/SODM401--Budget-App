About Us

Empowering DUT Students

SmartShopper AI was created to help DUT students, especially those in university residences, manage their monthly NSFAS allowances more effectively. We understand the challenges of budgeting while trying to get the best value for your money.

Our AI-powered platform uses advanced algorithms to search across multiple stores, compare prices, and provide personalized recommendations based on your shopping history and preferences.
